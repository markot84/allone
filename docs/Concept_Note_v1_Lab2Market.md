# Concept Note v.1 — Lab2Market

**Project:** AI-Powered Commercialization Support for Technology Transfer Offices  
**Call:** HORIZON-EIE-2026-03-CONNECT-01 (From lab to market: Strengthening the role of TTOs)  
**Lead concept:** Consortium with Not The Same as Tech/Commercial Partner  
**Date:** March 2026

---

## Challenge & Scope

Technology Transfer Offices (TTOs) face a structural gap: they manage portfolios of innovations (patents, spin-offs, licences) but lack systematic, data-driven tools to prioritise commercialisation opportunities and align internal teams with market-readiness pathways. Decisions on which innovations to push to market often rely on ad-hoc processes, spreadsheets and expert judgement — leading to misalignment, slower time-to-market and underutilised research outputs.

The EU policy priority **Fit for the Digital Age** and the **European Innovation Ecosystems** work programme aim to strengthen the capacity of TTOs to bring knowledge to the market. This call explicitly targets coordination and support actions that enhance TTO capabilities.

## Your Idea / Intervention

An **AI-powered Commercialization Support Platform** that TTOs can use to:

1. **Prioritise innovations** — Apply a methodology inspired by commercial intelligence (portfolio scoring, strategy weights) to rank spin-offs/patents by market-readiness, scalability potential and commercial fit.
2. **Automate decision support** — Use AI (e.g. Google Generative AI, custom prompts) to generate market-fit scenarios, scaling recommendations and commercial policy options tailored to each innovation.
3. **Coordinate workflows** — Provide dashboards and shared workspaces so TTO staff, researchers and industry partners align on commercialisation milestones.
4. **Train TTO staff** — Deliver workshops and modules on data-driven commercialisation, strategic prioritisation and AI-assisted market assessment.

**Not The Same** contributes as Tech/Commercial Partner: platform components (adapted from Performance+ methodology), training design and delivery, analytics and data integration capabilities.

## Strategic Objectives

1. **Strengthen TTO commercialisation capacity** — Equip TTOs with practical, AI-assisted tools and methods for prioritisation and market-readiness assessment.
2. **Demonstrate impact** — Pilot the platform with 2–3 TTOs across different countries/sectors and document uptake, learning and scalability.
3. **Create reusable assets** — Guidelines, methodologies and open modules that other TTOs can adopt beyond the project.

## Expected Results (by end of project)

- A functional **Commercialisation Support Platform** (prototype or adapted SaaS) used by participating TTOs.
- **Training materials and workshops** delivered to TTO staff.
- **Pilot reports** documenting uptake, feedback and lessons learned.
- **Guidelines and best practices** for AI-assisted commercialisation workflows, suitable for dissemination to the TTO community.

## Consortium (Target)

- **Coordinator:** TTO or RTO with Horizon experience
- **TTO Partner(s):** 1–2 university TTOs with innovation portfolios
- **Tech/Commercial Partner:** Not The Same (AI, training, analytics)
- **End-user / Pilot:** SME or mid-cap for validation

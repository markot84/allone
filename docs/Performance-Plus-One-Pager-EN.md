# Performance+ — One Pager

## Commercial Intelligence & Execution OS

---

### The Problem

Growing companies (€2-50M revenue) face a structural problem: commercial data lives in ERPs, ad consoles and spreadsheets; strategic decisions happen in meetings and emails; execution is fragmented across internal departments and external partners. The result: misalignment, slower decision-making, targeting errors and wasted budget.

### The Solution

Performance+ is the first Commercial Intelligence & Execution OS that closes the full cycle — from raw data to commercial intelligence to coordinated execution — in a single AI-powered system.

It ingests transactional, advertising and financial data, transforms it into actionable business intelligence, and produces commercial policy scenarios, channel recommendations and content directions. In parallel, it aligns management, internal departments and external partners in a shared decision-making framework, eliminating information fragmentation and communication delays that slow down growth.

### Data Input — Zero Setup Time

Performance+ works with or without API integrations. Data can be imported via CSV, manual entry or API from day one:

| Source | Data Types |
|---|---|
| **ERP** | Products, inventory, pricing, customer records, transactions |
| **Google Ads** | Campaign performance, cost, conversions, audience data |
| **Meta Ads** | Campaign performance, audiences, Meta-IDs, attribution |

### Buyer Personas & Go-to-Market

Performance+ serves three distinct buyer personas with different buying motions:

**1. Marketing Agency (Primary Buyer)**
- Manages 10-50 brands simultaneously — multi-tenant architecture maximizes LTV
- Needs a framework to justify decisions to clients — Performance+ becomes their competitive advantage
- Selling to 1 agency = reaching 20+ brands
- Positioning: "The system behind your agency's strategy"

**2. Marketing Manager / Commercial Director (Internal Champion)**
- Daily user who drives adoption within the organization
- Doesn't hold budget authority but can influence the CEO
- Needs free tier or trial to prove value before requesting budget
- Positioning: "Replace spreadsheets and guesswork with data-driven commercial policy"

**3. Growth Consultant (Force Multiplier)**
- Works with 3-10 companies simultaneously
- Needs a framework, not just advice — Performance+ becomes their operating system
- Positioning: "Structured commercial intelligence for every client engagement"

The business owner/CEO is the **beneficiary** but rarely the direct buyer. They trust their agency, marketing manager, or consultant to evaluate and recommend tools.
